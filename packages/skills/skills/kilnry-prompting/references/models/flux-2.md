# FLUX.2 (image, image edit)

A sharp, controllable image model good for product and design work.

- Describe the subject precisely; FLUX.2 rewards concrete nouns over mood words.
- Name the light and the surface (studio softbox, hard sun, matte, glossy).
- It edits cleanly: state the change and keep the instruction short so the rest stays.
- Give the aspect ratio and, for product work, a plain background if you want one.
- Negative-direction clutter and text when they are not wanted.
