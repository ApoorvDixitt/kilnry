# Seedance (text-to-video, image-to-video)

A motion model. Write a single continuous shot, not a montage.

- Open with the subject and the setting, then name one camera move (push in, pan left, orbit) and hold it for the whole clip.
- Describe motion in the scene — what moves, how fast — rather than cuts; Seedance renders one take.
- Keep it to five to ten seconds of action. Ask for more and it compresses.
- For image-to-video, the reference frame is the first frame: prompt only the motion that leaves it.
- Avoid on-screen text and rapid style changes; both smear.
