# Kling (text-to-video, image-to-video)

A cinematic motion model with strong physics and faces.

- Lead with the shot: size (wide, medium, close), lens feel, and one camera move.
- State the light and the mood in a few words; Kling leans filmic and rewards it.
- One action per clip. A subject doing one thing well beats a list of beats.
- For image-to-video, keep the described motion consistent with the framing of the reference.
- Negative-direction the usual failures: no warping hands, no extra limbs, no text.
