# MiniMax H3 (text-to-video)

A motion model Kilnry drives with its H3 prompt system: build each clip as a ten-second block in three beats.

- **Hook (0–3 s)**: open on the subject with the strongest image; establish the setting fast.
- **Heart (3–7 s)**: carry the one point of the beat — the action, the reveal, the product in use.
- **Hand-off (7–10 s)**: resolve and lead into the next block so scenes cut on the beat.
- Name one camera move for the whole block; do not change style mid-block.
- Write one H3 block per scene; the motion-design workflow chains them at ten seconds each.
- No on-screen text; burn titles in assembly instead.
