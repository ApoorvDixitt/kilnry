# Script craft for a UGC ad

Write the spoken line before the visual. A creator ad earns attention in the first two seconds, so open on the hook — a problem the viewer feels or a result they want — not on the brand. Keep each segment to what one breath carries: roughly two and a half words per second of clip.

Structure by duration. A fifteen-second ad is one board: hook, the main point, and a close. Longer ads add a setup and a payoff before the close. Every segment names one idea; do not stack claims.

Speak only approved claims. If the brief lists what may be said about the product, the script may not imply anything beyond it — no "clinically proven", no "number one", no invented percentages. When the mode is product-only the creator's voice narrates over the product; when a person is on camera the line is theirs to say naturally.

Close with one action. Tell the viewer the single next step and stop; a UGC ad that asks for three things converts on none.
