# Look: documentary

A grounded, photographic look for explainer and list channels. Natural daylight, real interiors and locations, shallow depth of field, muted colour. Stills read as photographs, not illustrations. Captions sit in the lower third in a clean sans-serif. Motion, when used, is a slow push or a gentle parallax — never a whip or a spin.
