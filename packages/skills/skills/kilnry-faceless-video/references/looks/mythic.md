# Look: mythic

A painterly, atmospheric look for story, myth and lore channels. Chiaroscuro light, deep shadow, gold and slate palette, textured brushwork. Stills suggest rather than state; faces are turned or in silhouette. Captions are centred in a serif face and fade rather than cut. Motion is a slow drift and a light grain, holding each still long enough to breathe.
