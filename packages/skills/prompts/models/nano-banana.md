# Nano Banana 2 and Pro

Both take up to 14 reference images (Pro: about five people plus objects and style images) and follow natural-language instructions well. Label references in the first line ("IMAGE REFERENCES: image 1 = Maya, image 2 = product") and refer to them positionally. Say what to keep ("keep her face, hair and nose stud exactly as in image 1") and what to change. Pro is the choice for face and outfit fusion and sheets; 2 is the value pick. Resolution 0.5K–4K; 4K doubles the price. No negative prompt or seed; write exclusions as instructions. Identity blurs past two people in practice; use close-ups per person when you need three or more.

Example 1: "IMAGE REFERENCES: image 1 = Maya, image 2 = serum bottle. The woman in image 1 holds the bottle from image 2 at chest height in a bright bathroom, morning light, natural skin texture, no text, no watermark. Keep her face, hair and nose stud exactly as in image 1."

Example 2: "Using the person in image 1, make a 3 by 3 grid of head-and-shoulders portraits with identical framing: neutral, gentle smile, laughing, surprised, angry, sad, thoughtful, determined, shouting. Mid-grey background, soft frontal light, no labels."
