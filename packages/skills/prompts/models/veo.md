# Veo 3.1

Veo 3.1 makes 4, 6 or 8 s clips in 16:9 or 9:16 at 720p–4K with strong physics, native audio, and up to three reference images (asset references force 8 s). Describe the scene like a cinematographer: shot size, lens feel, movement, light quality, then the action, then sound. Veo follows dialogue in quotes and adds ambience when asked. Negative prompts and seeds are supported. Keep people descriptions specific but short; Veo does not accept identity tokens, so Kilnry passes reference images plus a short descriptor. Person-generation rules vary by region; with people in image-to-video use adult subjects. Audio-filter false positives happen; one retry is reasonable. Standard is $0.40/s with audio; fast is $0.15/s on fal and is the default route.

Example 1: "Wide shot, 35 mm look, golden hour. A woman matching the reference images walks across a rooftop terrace toward the parapet, wind in her hair, city haze behind. Slow push-in. Distant traffic and a soft breeze, no music."

Example 2: "Macro, shallow focus. Chai pours from a steel kettle into a glass, steam curling, cardamom pods on a brass tray. Natural pour and clink sounds. 8 seconds."
