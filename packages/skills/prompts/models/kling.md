# Kling 3.0

Kling 3.0 renders 3–15 s at 720p (standard) or 1080p (pro), with optional native audio and multi-shot prompts (total no more than 15 s). Its strength is subject consistency through elements: Kilnry sends a Character element, so refer to it exactly the way Kilnry injects it and describe action, not appearance. Camera language works well ("slow dolly in", "orbit", "whip pan"). Use the negative prompt for artefacts ("blur, distortion, extra fingers, text"). For speech, Kling voices are attached by Kilnry; only Kling-created voices qualify. Elements roughly double the per-second price; audio on adds about 50 percent. Image-to-video ignores the aspect ratio and follows the input image.

Example 1: "The character element walks toward camera along a rain-wet street at dusk, neon reflections underfoot, slow dolly back keeping her centred, confident half-smile, city ambience, no dialogue."

Example 2 (multi-shot): "Shot 1 (5 s): wide, the character element sits at a café table stirring chai. Shot 2 (5 s): close-up of the glass from image 2, steam rising. Shot 3 (5 s): she looks up and laughs. Warm afternoon light throughout."
