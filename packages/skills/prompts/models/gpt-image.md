# GPT Image 2.5

GPT Image 2.5 is the sharpest model for multi-panel sheets, exact framing, legible typography, and instruction-heavy edits with up to 16 reference images at fixed high input fidelity. Sizes are free-form multiples of 16 up to 3840 px; quality low to max sets the token cost (medium about $0.04–0.05, high about $0.17–0.21). Reference images add about $0.024 each. Describe layout precisely ("four equal panels in one row, no borders") and state exact strings for any text in quotes. It renders text reliably; ask for "no text" when you do not want any. Transparent backgrounds are supported. No seed or negative prompt.

Example 1: "Using the person in image 1 as the exact same character, four equal panels in one row: front view, three-quarter left, left profile, back view. Waist-up, neutral pose, identical outfit and hair. Flat mid-grey studio background, soft even light, no text, no borders, photorealistic."

Example 2: "Poster, 2:3. Headline exactly 'CHAI DIARIES' in tall condensed sans at the top, subline 'Season two, every Sunday' in small caps below. Below the text, the woman in image 1 laughing with a glass of chai, warm tones, film grain, printed paper texture."
