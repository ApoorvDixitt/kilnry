# Security policy

## Supported versions

The latest minor release of Kilnry is supported. Older versions receive no fixes.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting on `ApoorvDixitt/kilnry`, or email `security@kilnry.app`. Do not open public issues for security bugs.

## Scope

The Kilnry app, launcher, Docker image, and documentation site are in scope. Provider APIs and third-party models are out of scope.

## Response targets

We aim to acknowledge reports within three business days, triage within seven days, and provide a fix or mitigation within 30 days for high or critical issues and 90 days otherwise. Coordinated disclosure follows the fix, with credit when requested. This bootstrapped project does not offer a bounty programme.

## Safe harbour

Good-faith research on your own installation is welcome. Do not test against other people's machines.

## Diagnostics

Nothing is sent from your installation to Kilnry. Settings › About › Export diagnostics produces a local bundle with keys and tokens removed.
