# Third-party notices

Kilnry depends on third-party packages and tools under their original licences. The generated dependency inventory will be refreshed for every release.

## Runtime and build dependencies

Node.js, Next.js, React, TypeScript, PGlite, Drizzle, pg-boss, AI SDK, MCP SDK, Tailwind CSS, Radix, Motion, Vitest, Playwright, and their transitive dependencies retain their respective licences. Exact package names, versions, copyrights, and licence identifiers will be generated from `pnpm-lock.yaml` before each release.

## Fonts

Geist and Geist Mono are used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and are self-hosted by Kilnry.

## FFmpeg

FFmpeg is invoked as a separate system process. Kilnry prefers a system FFmpeg 7 installation and may download a checksum-pinned platform build. The corresponding source and licence notices are distributed with release artifacts.
